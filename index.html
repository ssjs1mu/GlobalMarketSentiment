JC's Market Sentiment Tracker — quotes refresh automatically every 30 s from Yahoo Finance public endpoints (some exchanges delayed up to 15 min) · Fear & Greed every 10 min from CNN Business · FX reference fallback every 30 min from ECB / Frankfurter · ticker tape by TradingView (continuous).
Every block shows its own auto-refresh interval and last-updated time — use the ⟳ Refresh Now button (or press R) to refresh all feeds on demand.
If a feed can't be reached directly from the browser, the page automatically retries through public CORS relays and flags any stale values in amber — no simulated numbers are ever shown.
Note: FII/DII cash flows and Nifty option-chain PCR are published by NSE through endpoints that block browser access — restoring them needs a tiny server-side proxy/broker API.
